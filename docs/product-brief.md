# Product Brief

## Purpose

Meal Ledger is a simple, private daily journal for food, water intake, and food spending.

It should help a user answer:

- How much did I spend on food today, this week, or this month?
- Roughly how many calories did I eat?
- Did I reach my daily water goal?
- When did I eat and drink during the day?

## Target User

People who want a lightweight way to track eating, hydration, and food spending without an account, ads, or a complicated nutrition database.

## MVP

- Log a food entry: name, time, meal type, portion note, estimated calories, and price.
- Log a water entry: amount and time.
- Show today's food, water, calorie, and spend totals.
- Let users set currency, a water goal, and an optional calorie target.
- Persist all data locally on the device.

## Non-Goals for Version 1

- Accounts or cloud sync
- Barcode scanning
- Automatic nutrition lookup
- Meal photos
- Social features
- Subscriptions or ads

## Product Principles

- Fast enough to log an entry in a few seconds.
- Offline-first and private by default.
- Keep calorie tracking approximate rather than pretending to be medically precise.
- Make water logging especially quick.
