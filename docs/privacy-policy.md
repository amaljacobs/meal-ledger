# Meal Ledger Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-16

Meal Ledger stores the food, water, calorie, protein, spending, and preference information entered in the app only on the user's device.

The MVP does not create accounts, transmit data to a server, use analytics, display advertising, or share personal data with third parties. Removing the app or clearing its app storage removes its locally stored data.

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the repository: https://github.com/amaljacobs/meal-ledger
